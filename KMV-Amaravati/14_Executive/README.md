## The three corrections that move a number

| | Correction | Effect |
| --- | --- | --: |
| **1** | **Reclassification.** ₹31.75 Cr is four kinds of money under one label | Marketing opex **₹23.80 Cr** |
| **2** | **The missing headcount line.** The model carried agency fees and no internal team | Envelope rises to **₹25.33–26.86 Cr** |
| **3** | **The missing cancellation allowance.** 376 bookings were treated as 376 units sold | **409 gross → 376 net**; base case **₹466.2 Cr** |

**Correction 2 raises the ask, and correction 3 lowers the revenue.** Both are stated because a budget that
appears complete is harder to trust than one that admits what it is missing.

## What the document will not do

- **No estimate of the developer's actual budget, spend, headcount, historical CPL, sales velocity or
  inventory position.** None is available; naming the gap beats inventing the number.
- **No manufactured industry benchmark.** Published rate bands are used and cited; where none exists, a range
  is labelled as an assumption.
- **No ROI stated as revenue ÷ spend.** That ratio reads **16.0×** and is called out as meaningless. ROMI is
  **(gross profit − marketing) ÷ marketing** — **3.47× at an assumed 28% margin.**
