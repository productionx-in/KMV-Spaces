## The question the deck answers

> *If you were responsible for marketing this project, how would you take it to market, whom would you target, how would you position the four communities, how would you build demand, how would you allocate and govern the marketing investment, and how would you measure performance and ROI?*

---

## The argument in six positions

1. **The opportunity is real, and narrower than it looks.** Amaravati's capital status is now statutory and capital works are funded and active — but of 300+ listed corridor projects, **eleven** genuinely compete, and the corridor is crowded at the bottom and thin at the top.
2. **Four communities are four businesses**, differentiated by buyer constraint rather than finish level. The binding constraint is **cash at purchase, not income** — ₹17.6 lakh for A rising to ₹74.1 lakh for D.
3. **The position must be provable.** "Luxury", "premium" and "close to the capital" are universal in this corridor and therefore worthless.
4. **₹31.75 Cr is not a marketing budget.** It bundles four economically different things. True marketing operating expense is **₹23.80 Cr**; media is **₹14.65 Cr — ₹32.6 lakh a month**.
5. **Site-visit conversion, not media price, is the lever.** One point of site-visit-to-booking is worth **₹39.4 Cr** at zero additional media.
6. **So the money is not released on a forecast.** The ask is **₹25 lakh** — about 1% of the envelope — with the stop condition agreed before it moves.

> **Marketing investment should be earned by performance, not committed by forecast.**

## Source discipline

**Four labels are used on every slide and never blurred:**

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Assignment assumption** | Handed to me in the brief |
| **Researched** | Externally sourced, with a `[Sxx]` reference |
| **Derived calculation** | My own arithmetic on stated inputs |
| **Working assumption** | Assumed because the brief did not provide it — **requires KMV validation** |

**Every content slide carries a source strip** naming its sources, its basis, and what would falsify or replace it. That is a structural element, not a decoration — it is the deck's central discipline made visible.

> **Nothing in this deck has been confirmed by KMV Spaces.** The exact location, product mix, launch dates, approvals, final pricing, inventory, construction status and commercial assumptions were not provided in the brief.

### The limitation that governs every citation

**Search worked. Page retrieval did not.** Every URL in the register is real and resolves; **not one was opened**. A Tier 1 domain means the domain is authoritative — not that the document was verified. Appendix G states this in the deck itself.

## What the deck deliberately does not do

- **It does not claim the AIIMS credential.** The Ministry's own tender names HSCC as executing agency `[S05]`, and the group's page does not define scope `[S17]`. The position falls back to the density arithmetic and an ungated price sheet — both provable without any credential.
- **It does not mention the airport or a metro.** Two searches found no DPR or notification for either.
- **It does not present the ORR as an amenity.** It is *approved, with land acquisition begun* `[S09]` — never *coming*.
- **It does not present revenue ÷ spend as ROI.** That ratio reads 16.0× and appears once, labelled meaningless.
- **It does not estimate KMV's actual budget, spend, headcount, cost per lead, sales velocity or inventory.** None was available; each is named as a gap.
