| --- | --- |

## 3. The five that would change the strategy if wrong

| Assumption | Exposure |
| --- | --- |
| **A6 — gross margin at 28%** | Every ROI, ROMI, break-even and CAC-ceiling figure rests on it. It is a placeholder |
| **A3 / A4 — the price ladder** | A **50% premium to the Mangalagiri locality asking average**, untested. Up to **₹50.7 Cr** of price risk |
| **A12 — visit-to-booking at 6.9%** | **₹39.4 Cr per point.** The single highest-leverage number in the model |
| **A14 — cancellation at 8%** | **₹40.5 Cr.** The base case delivers ₹466.2 Cr, not the ₹506.7 Cr plan target |
| **A20 — segment sizing and behaviour** | The entire targeting plan, and ₹121.5 Cr allocated against the largest segment alone |

## 4. Derived figures and how each was computed

| Figure | Computation |
| --- | --- |
| Density 15.0/acre | 376 units ÷ 25 acres |
| Marketing opex ₹23.80 Cr | ₹31.75 Cr − ₹4.04 Cr cost of sale − ₹3.15 Cr sales capex − ₹0.76 Cr technology capex |
| Media per month ₹32.6 L | ₹14.65 Cr ÷ 45 months |
| CPL ₹1,247 | ₹9.84 Cr lead-generation media ÷ 78,880 leads |
| CPQL ₹7,889 | ₹9.84 Cr ÷ 12,474 qualified leads |
| Cost per booked visit ₹20,205 | ₹9.84 Cr ÷ 4,870 booked visits |
| **Cost per qualified site visit ₹33,676** | ₹9.84 Cr ÷ 2,922 visits **held** |
| Site visits booked 4,870 | 2,922 held ÷ 60% show rate |
| Gross bookings 409 | 376 net ÷ (1 − 8% cancellation) |
| Marketing cost per unit ₹6.33 L | ₹23.80 Cr ÷ 376 net units |
| **CAC ₹8.44 L** | ₹31.75 Cr ÷ 376 net units |
| Maximum acceptable CAC ₹11.32 L | 30% of gross profit per unit at a 28% margin |
| Funnel ceiling headroom 1.34× | ₹11.32 L ÷ ₹8.44 L |
| Gross profit ₹141.9 Cr | ₹506.7 Cr × 28% |
| **ROMI 3.47×** | (₹141.9 Cr − ₹31.75 Cr) ÷ ₹31.75 Cr — **on gross profit, not revenue** |
| Break-even 84 units | ₹31.75 Cr ÷ ₹37.73 L gross profit per unit |
| Incremental return 2.65 | 1 crore ÷ ₹37.73 L gross profit per unit |
| Revenue ÷ spend 16.0× | ₹506.7 Cr ÷ ₹31.75 Cr — **shown only to be rejected as meaningless** |

## 5. What was never established, anywhere behind this work

- **No primary buyer research.** No surveys, interviews, focus groups, CRM data or walk-in analysis. Every behavioural attribute across eight segments is hypothesis.
- **No transaction data.** Every price quoted is an **asking** price. Registration data was never obtained.
- **No construction cost data.** Margin, and therefore every ROI figure, rests on a placeholder.
- **No RERA record and no established research report** was retrieved — the two source tiers that would settle pricing, absorption and legitimacy.
- **No verified 2026 headcount** for people working in Amaravati. The only figures found are 2016 reporting.
- **No estimate is offered anywhere** for KMV's actual marketing budget, spend, headcount, historical cost per lead, sales velocity or inventory position. None was available, and inventing one would be worse than naming the gap.
