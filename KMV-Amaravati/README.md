**Phases 1–12 were built without the ability to retrieve a single web page.** Phase 13 was the first with
working search, and it did not confirm the earlier work — **it contradicted it in six places, four of which
weaken the plan.** Full detail in [`12_Strategy/`](12_Strategy/).

| # | Finding | Effect |
| --: | --- | --- |

| **2** | **KMV Vivaan is at Poranki, not on the corridor** — and runs 9.2 units/acre, not 6.6 | The density precedent was overstated by 39% |

## Method notes

**Two kinds of gap.** [`11_Gaps/`](11_Gaps/) separates gaps in the *market* — things nobody is doing that this
project could do — from gaps in *this plan* — assumptions made and never tested. They read alike and demand
opposite responses. **Twenty-four of thirty-six carry an exposure, and twelve of those cost nothing new to
close.** Eighteen of thirty-six close for **₹41.5 lakh**, 1.3% of the marketing budget, and they include every
one of the five largest exposures.

**The budget was then audited adversarially, and it did not survive intact.**
[`08_Funnel_Economics/investment-model-audit.md`](08_Funnel_Economics/investment-model-audit.md) finds the
₹31.75 crore is **a taxonomy error, not a magnitude error**: it bundles marketing opex, cost of sale, sales
capex and technology capex under one label. True marketing operating expense is **₹23.80 crore**, of which
media is **₹14.65 crore — ₹32.6 lakh a month across 45 months**. Two further findings: the budget **breaches
the ₹6.3 lakh per-unit allowable** that Phase 5 set and Phase 7 respected, by 34% — and the reclassified
figure lands at **₹6.33 lakh, within 0.5% of that ceiling**, so the overshoot and the misclassified money are
the same ₹8 crore. Sensitivity also reverses an earlier headline: **CPL is the biggest risk, but site-visit
conversion is the biggest opportunity**, and media is the most expensive and least controllable place to buy
a booking.

**The decision document then raised the ask and lowered the revenue in the same breath.**
[`14_Executive/marketing-investment-growth-strategy.md`](14_Executive/marketing-investment-growth-strategy.md)
finds two further omissions the audit had not reached. The model carried **agency fees and no internal
marketing headcount** — adding a labelled range for two people over 51 months takes the envelope *up* to
**₹25.33–26.86 crore**. And the funnel carried **no cancellation allowance at all**: 376 bookings were treated
as 376 units sold, so **409 gross bookings are needed to net 376**, and the base case delivers **346 net units
and ₹466.2 crore — a ₹40.5 crore shortfall against the plan's own target.** The verdict is **conditional**:
approve a governed envelope, **release ₹25 lakh**, and ratify the kill condition before the money moves.

**Bottom-up budgeting, and forcing it to reconcile.** [`08_Funnel_Economics/`](08_Funnel_Economics/) prices each
budget line as *quantity × unit cost* and only then compares to the top-down allocation. The two disagree by
**28%**, and the gap sits almost entirely in things that are not media. Requiring the variance table to sum to
the variance is what caught a dropped line: the ₹0.70 Cr referral programme had fallen out of the build while
the CAC table still charged it against 70 bookings. **A decomposition that does not add up to the difference it
explains is not a decomposition.**

**Evidence grading, applied strictly.** Infrastructure claims are graded CONFIRMED / UNDER CONSTRUCTION /
APPROVED / PROPOSED / REPORTED / SPECULATIVE ([`02_Infrastructure/`](02_Infrastructure/)). Buyer research
separates FACT / OBSERVATION / INFERENCE / RECOMMENDATION and records unverifiable items as *"insufficient
public evidence"* rather than inferring weakness from absence. Phase 13 adds the brief's six-column evidence
system — **only 5 of 77 rows are FACT, and 11 record something searched for and not found.**

**Bottom-up pricing.** [`03_Pricing/`](03_Pricing/) derives every ₹/sq ft figure as *published ticket ÷
published unit size* for named, RERA-registered projects, rather than quoting locality averages — because
published averages for the same micro-market disagree by up to 40%. Seven of the eight projects publishing both
ends of a range agree internally within 0.7%.

**Self-correction is the method's only real evidence.** Six phases corrected an earlier phase's conclusion:
Phase 3 found three of Phase 1's claimed advantages false; Phase 5 cut the sales task from ~600 units to ~397;
Phase 6 rebuilt the community architecture rather than accepting the assumed tiering; Phase 9 corrected Phase
8's referral count; Phase 11 caught its own reconciliation failing to close; and Phase 12 found the density
claim in Phase 10's signature line is false against two named villa competitors unless bounded. **A sequence
that never contradicts itself is not being applied.**

## The limitation that governs everything

**Search worked. Page retrieval did not.** Every fetch attempt returned `EGRESS_BLOCKED`, and direct requests to
`crda.ap.gov.in` and `rera.ap.gov.in` returned `403` policy denials.

> **The forty URLs in [`13_Sources/`](13_Sources/) are real and they resolve — but not one of them was opened.**
> Every citation is search-surfaced rather than read. **A Tier 1 domain means the domain is authoritative, not
> that the document was verified.**

**Tiers 2 and 4 — RERA and established research — returned nothing at all**, which is precisely why
[`03_Pricing/`](03_Pricing/) cannot escape asking prices and [`04_Competitors/`](04_Competitors/) cannot
establish velocity.

**This is why [`13_Sources/source-register.md`](13_Sources/source-register.md) ends in a twenty-item
verification checklist rather than a bibliography.** The four items at the top of it carry more risk than the
other sixteen combined, and three conversations close them: **KMV on the AIIMS work order and the Vivaan booking
book; a records agent on 24 months of IGRS registrations; a mystery shopper on two villa projects.**
