## 1. The method, and why it was chosen

**Published locality averages for this micro-market disagree by up to 40%.** So every ₹/sq ft figure in this exercise is derived as:

> **published ticket price ÷ published unit size**, for named, RERA-registered projects

**Seven of the eight projects that publish both ends of a range agree internally within 0.7%**, which is why the derived bands are preferred over any locality average.

**Phase 13 tested this method against external data and it held** — the two project-level figures it could check corroborated the approach.

### 3.1 What the comparison says

> **The plan asks a 50% premium to the Mangalagiri locality average and 11% to the micromarket.**

**That is not automatically wrong.** A 15-units-per-acre scheme with villas *should* price above a locality average dominated by apartments. **But three things follow:**

1. **The premium is now sized, and it never was before.** The ladder was built bottom-up from competitor tickets and never asked *"how far above the locality are we?"*
2. **Community A is the exposure nobody flagged.** At ₹5,150 it prices **above Manjeera Monarch at ₹5,100** — a delivered apartment project in the same locality — and **27% above the Mangalagiri average.** A is 169 units, **45% of the scheme**, and the community least able to justify a premium, because a garden apartment competes directly on rate.
3. **Community D may be *under*-priced.** KMV Vivaan asks **₹3.59 Cr (Ph I) and ₹5.21 Cr (Ph II)**. If those clear, D at ₹2.30–2.74 Cr sits **below the developer's own achieved range.**

**Observations 2 and 3 point in opposite directions, and both are testable from data the developer already holds.**

## 4. Where sources conflict

| Series | Figure | Strength | Weakness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mangalagiri locality | ₹4,062 | Locality-specific; the correct geography | Asking prices; **drifting down while its own micromarket rises** |
| Micromarket | ₹5,489 | Rising trend, consistent with capital activity | **Geography undefined** — may include Tadepalli and Patamata, both higher |
| **Project-level** | **₹4,210–5,100** | **Named, specific, checkable** | Only three projects; all asking |

> **Verdict: the project-level figures are strongest**, because they are named and checkable. **The locality average is weakest**, because it aggregates unknown product mix. The same portal reports the locality **falling** (₹4,098 → ₹4,062) while the micromarket **rises** (₹4,805 → ₹5,489) — a contradiction that deserves scrutiny before either figure is used.
>
> **What should be treated as uncertain: all of it.** Every figure in this file is an asking price.

## 5. The unresolved question, on its eighth phase

**Is the ₹2.2–2.8 crore band occupied?**

Phase 2 asked it. Phases 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11 and 12 each carried it forward. **Phase 13 searched for it directly and still found no project-level evidence of depth.**

Seven phases of inability to find evidence, plus one direct search that also found none, is **weak evidence of absence** — not proof, but enough that the burden has shifted. **₹268 crore of the plan is priced into that band.**

**Two things would settle it, and neither is expensive:**

| Action | Cost | Settles |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ask KMV what Vivaan actually sold for, and how fast** | **Free** | Whether the developer already clears the band |
| Mystery-shop TAG AIRA and Prime Grandeur, 8 weeks | ~₹3.5 L | Competitor absorption in the band |
| IGRS registration series, 24 months, 2 mandals | ~₹2.5 L | **Achieved vs asking, across the whole ladder** |

## 6. What replaces this file

**IGRS AP registration data for Mangalagiri and Thullur mandals** — a 24-month series cut by ticket band and unit size.

**It is the single highest-value missing source in the entire repository**, named independently by both Phase 1 and Phase 2 before Phase 13 quantified what it would settle: **up to ₹50.7 crore of price risk.**

**Decision rule once obtained:** set the published sheet within 5% of the observed 75th percentile, and treat any band where achieved sits >12% below asking as a re-cut candidate.
