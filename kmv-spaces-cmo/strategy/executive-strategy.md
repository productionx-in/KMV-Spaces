# Executive Strategy

## THE FIVE BIGGEST PROBLEMS

**1. The company's best asset is invisible.**
Two first-in-category green certifications — India's first Net Zero Energy villa community and AP's first IGBC Platinum pre-certification **[VERIFIED]** — with no regional competitor making an equivalent claim. There is no indexed blog, no PR engine, and no sustained content programme carrying that story. **Rare product substance generating almost no demand.**

**2. Brand equity is trapped one level too low.**
454 Justdial ratings for *Vivaan*; 33 Google reviews for *KMV Spaces* **[VERIFIED]**. A separate Vivaan microsite and Facebook page. TRAYA launching as *"Makers of Vivaan."* **[INFERENCE]** Every new project restarts credibility from near zero — a tax paid on all four projects and every one that follows.

**3. The brand is fragmented across seven-plus identities.**
Three Facebook pages, two Instagram accounts — one dead with **481 followers and zero posts** — a near-dormant X account, and a separate microsite **[OBSERVED]**. Audience, ad-pixel data, reviews and search authority all split. **Cost to fix: near zero. Currently unfixed.**

**4. The commercial funnel is unmeasured, and possibly unmeasurable.**
Whether a CRM exists, what speed-to-lead is, what any campaign has produced — all *Not publicly available*. **[INFERENCE]** Without a closed loop from source to booking, every budget decision is guesswork, and the reverse-funnel model shows a **29× swing in required media spend** between a weak and a strong funnel.

**5. Distribution is working around the company, not for it.**
A broker published TRAYA's pre-launch details before KMV's own channels did **[OBSERVED]**. **[INFERENCE]** The largest unmanaged commercial relationship in the business, at a moment when narrative control determines launch pricing power.

## THE FIVE STRATEGIC MOVES

### MOVE 1 — Consolidate the brand into one compounding asset
Retire duplicate identities; migrate kmvvivaan.com to kmvspaces.com/vivaan with redirects; adopt endorsed architecture — *"KMV Spaces presents TRAYA."*
**Why:** makes every future launch cheaper. **Cost:** near zero. **Impact:** compounding.

### MOVE 2 — Rebuild the funnel before scaling spend
CRM with single-source-of-truth capture; **<5 minute speed-to-lead**; dedicated landing pages under 2 seconds; MQL/SQL definitions and an SLA with sales; closed-loop booking attribution.
**Why:** **[VERIFIED]** 5-minute response = 4× site-visit conversion; sub-2s pages cut CPL 20–35%. **Impact:** largest single lever in the business.

### MOVE 3 — Turn the certifications into a demand engine
Quantified running-cost content, resident bill case studies, a savings calculator, honest capital-region intelligence, PR, and founder thought leadership connected to HEALTHWAY.
**Why:** converts the monopoly asset into search visibility, price justification, and answers the verified "expensive" objection with arithmetic.

### MOVE 4 — Launch TRAYA on proof, not speculation
Position as *proven builders, future-proof homes, in the capital region.* Lead with delivery evidence and running-cost economics; demote capital appreciation to a supporting argument.
**Why:** **[INFERENCE]** competing on appreciation puts a ₹3 Cr villa against ₹40 lakh plots and loses. Competing on evidenced delivery is where KMV is uniquely strong.

### MOVE 5 — Professionalise the two owned distribution channels
Formalise channel partners (tiering, lead registration, kits, mandatory Vivaan site visit) and activate existing residents (referral programme, private previews, review generation).
**Why:** the two highest-converting, lowest-cost sources of bookings, both currently unmanaged.

## ONE-PAGE STRATEGY

### WHERE ARE WE?
A ten-year-old developer with genuinely rare product substance — India's first Net Zero Energy villa community, AP's first IGBC Platinum pre-certification, a completed community rated 4.2/5 across 454 ratings, and a parent group that builds AIIMS campuses and airports for the Government of India — and almost no commercial machinery converting any of it into demand. Brand equity sits in a project rather than the company, identity is scattered across seven-plus digital properties, and the funnel is unmeasured.

### WHERE DO WE WANT TO GO?
To be the **default premium residential brand of the Andhra capital region** — the developer a ₹3 crore buyer names first and trusts most — with a marketing function that reports bookings and cost per booking, not impressions.

### WHY?
Because the market is re-rating now (₹57,821 Cr of capital works live, Tadepalli +34.2% in three years, limited premium supply), competitors are arriving (Aparna is physically adjacent to TRAYA), and KMV holds a certification monopoly that no regional rival can answer this year. **Attention is about to become abundant and trust is about to become scarce.** KMV already owns the raw materials for trust and is not converting them. That window is measured in quarters.

### HOW?
1. **Consolidate** the brand so equity compounds instead of resetting
2. **Fix the funnel** — speed, qualification, landing pages, sales alignment — before scaling spend
3. **Harvest** existing residents and channel partners, the cheapest demand available
4. **Launch TRAYA** on proof and running-cost economics, not capital speculation
5. **Build category authority** in certified sustainable living while it is uncontested

### WHAT WILL WE MEASURE?
Bookings · Cost per booking · Site visits completed · Speed-to-lead · Qualified lead rate.
Reported weekly to leadership, monthly to promoters, against a closed loop from ad source to booked revenue.

## THE BOARDROOM TEST

Challenging the strategy against the hardest available questions.

**Is this actually differentiated?**
Yes — and verifiably so. The certifications are **[VERIFIED]** and uncontested regionally. The risk is not that the strategy is generic; it is that a competitor copies the *positioning* before KMV establishes it. That argues for speed, which is why brand consolidation and content start in month one.

**Is this commercially realistic?**
Yes. The largest moves — consolidating identities, fixing speed-to-lead, activating residents, formalising partners — cost process and discipline, not capital. Media spend is deliberately deferred until the funnel can convert it.

**Am I assuming data I don't have?**
Yes, and it is labelled throughout. All funnel rates are **[ASSUMPTION]**; benchmarks are cited as benchmarks; KMV's actual performance is stated as *Not publicly available*. The 90-day plan front-loads the audit that replaces assumptions with facts. **No budget should be committed on this document's numbers — only on its structure.**

**Am I recommending too many channels?**
This was the strategy's original weakness. Revised: only five channels are HIGH priority — referral, channel partners, Google Search, Meta, and the website/CRM. Display, influencer, print and radio are explicitly LOW. **[INFERENCE]** At ~60 bookings a year, channel focus beats channel coverage.

**Can management measure this?**
Not today — that is problem #4. The framework is deliberately sequenced so measurement exists by week 12, before significant spend scales.

**Can sales execute it?**
Unknown — sales capacity is *Not publicly available*, and it is a **hard ceiling** on the plan. Generating demand beyond capacity destroys conversion and wastes budget. This is the first thing to establish in week one.

**Can the company afford it?**
At 1.5–2% of revenue, marketing cost is comparable to or below typical channel-partner commission of 2%+. The plan is affordable *because* it prioritises unpaid levers first.

**Does it depend too heavily on Meta?**
No — deliberately. Meta is one of five HIGH channels, and Google Search is ranked above it despite higher CPL because **[VERIFIED]** its lead quality converts substantially better. Referral and channel partners rank above both. **A strategy that survives Meta CPMs doubling.**

**Does it work if lead costs increase?**
Better than the alternative. Rising CPLs hurt volume-dependent strategies most. This strategy's core levers — referral, partners, funnel conversion, organic authority — are largely insulated from auction inflation.

**Does it create long-term brand equity?**
Yes — that is Move 1's entire purpose. The endorsed architecture means every project launch adds to KMV Spaces rather than to an isolated project brand.

**Does it help move inventory?**
Yes, and it names the fastest: ready-to-move apartments — finished capital, zero delivery risk — currently receiving the least distinct marketing attention.

**Does it improve booking velocity?**
Directly: speed-to-lead, site-visit show rates, partner speed bonuses, and a structured site-visit experience all compress time-to-booking.

**Does it reduce dependence on individual projects?**
Yes — this is the central structural fix.

**Does it give leadership a clear ROI story?**
Yes: *"Every rupee is traceable from ad source to booked revenue, reported as cost per booking against a 1.5–2% marketing cost ratio."* That is the sentence the whole measurement architecture exists to make true.

### What the challenge changed
1. **Cut the channel list** — from a comprehensive evaluation to five HIGH-priority channels, with the rest explicitly deprioritised.
2. **Demoted capital-appreciation positioning** for TRAYA from a lead territory to a supporting argument, after concluding it puts a ₹3 Cr villa into an unwinnable fight against plots.
3. **Sequenced measurement before media** — deferring spend scale until week 12 rather than running both in parallel.
4. **Added the sales-capacity ceiling** as an explicit constraint rather than assuming marketing volume is the binding limit.
