# Content Strategy

## 1. The opportunity, stated commercially

**[OBSERVED]** KMV has a `/blogs` link in its footer with no indexed content, no non-brand search visibility, and a YouTube library of genuinely appropriate videos that almost nobody finds.

**[INFERENCE]** Regional competitor content is overwhelmingly project-promotional. Nobody owns *"how to evaluate a villa in the capital region," "what net-zero actually saves per year," "what to check before buying pre-launch in Amaravati."* In a market with ~10 premium villa projects, the volume of content required to become the authoritative voice is small. **Content authority here is purchasable with effort rather than budget** — which is precisely the kind of asymmetry a challenger brand should exploit.

| Pillar | Share | Funnel | Purpose |

### Pillar 1 — Certified sustainability & running-cost economics

**Purpose:** convert a certification nobody knows about into a commercial argument that justifies price.
**Audience:** all segments; especially price-objecting buyers.
**Formats:** long-form articles, calculator tool, resident bill case studies, video explainers.
**Platform:** website/SEO, YouTube, LinkedIn.
**CTA:** "Calculate what a net-zero home saves you."
**KPI:** organic sessions on cluster; calculator completions; assisted conversions.

**Concepts:**
- *"What Does a Net-Zero Home Actually Save You? We Added Up a Year of Bills."* — the single most valuable piece KMV could publish. Converts an abstract certification into rupees. **Requires real resident data.**
- *An interactive savings calculator* — current bill in, 20-year net-zero saving out. A genuine lead magnet, and the kind of tool that earns links and AI citations.
- *"IGBC Platinum vs Net Zero vs 'Eco-Friendly': What the Labels Mean"* — positions KMV as the honest expert while implicitly contrasting with competitors' vague green language. **[INFERENCE]** Powerful precisely because it is factually true and never names a competitor.
- *"Why We Put Solar on Every Roof in 2016"* — founder-voice origin story.

---

### Pillar 2 — Construction transparency & delivery proof

**Purpose:** neutralise the verified delivery-risk and possession-delay objections.
**Audience:** pre-launch buyers, booked customers, channel partners.
**Formats:** monthly progress films, drone footage, time-lapse, milestone posts.
**Platform:** YouTube, WhatsApp (to booked buyers), Instagram, website.
**CTA:** "Track construction monthly."
**KPI:** booked-customer engagement; objection frequency in sales calls.

**Concepts:**
- *Monthly "TRAYA Rising"* — dated drone progress series. Publishing progress publicly is the strongest possible answer to "will you deliver?"
- *"How We Build"* — foundation, materials, QC, using KMV Projects' institutional standards as the story.
- *"What Happened at Vivaan"* — **[INFERENCE] the boldest and most valuable piece here.** Honestly address the possession delay, explain the cause, and show what changed. A developer that publicly owns a past delay is far more credible than one that stays silent while reviews say it anyway.

---

### Pillar 3 — Location & capital-region intelligence

**Purpose:** capture top-of-funnel non-brand search; establish regional authority.
**Audience:** researchers, out-of-region and NRI buyers.
**Formats:** guides, data-led articles, maps, video explainers.
**Platform:** website/SEO, YouTube, AI answer engines.
**CTA:** "Get the Tadepalli buyer's guide."
**KPI:** non-brand organic sessions, keyword rankings, AI citations.

**Concepts:**
- *"The Tadepalli Buyer's Guide 2026"* — prices, appreciation, schools, hospitals, commute times, infrastructure status. **[VERIFIED]** data exists (₹5,653/sqft, +34.2% 3-yr) to make this genuinely authoritative.
- *"Amaravati: What's Actually Built, What's Under Construction, What's Only Announced."* **[INFERENCE]** The most trust-building piece available in this market. Every competitor is hyping; a developer that separates ₹57,821 Cr of live works from a still-unacquired ORR earns disproportionate credibility — and pre-empts the "will it stall again?" objection honestly.
- *"Villa vs Apartment vs Plot: What ₹3 Crore Buys in the Capital Region"* — directly reframes the plot competitor identified in `/research/competitors.md` §B.
- *"NRI's Guide to Buying in the Capital Region"* — process, FEMA, documentation, remote purchase.

### Pillar 4 — Resident stories & social proof

**Purpose:** convert consideration into visits and bookings.
**Audience:** all, especially late-funnel.
**Formats:** video testimonials, handover films, photo essays, community events.
**Platform:** YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, landing pages.
**CTA:** "Visit the community and meet residents."
**KPI:** video completion, landing-page conversion lift, site-visit bookings.

**Concepts:**
- *"Four Years In"* — residents who bought in 2020, on what they'd do differently. Unscripted credibility.
- *Handover films* — KMV already produces these well on Facebook. Systematise into a series.
- *"A Day at Vivaan"* — lifestyle rather than sales.
- **[RECOMMENDATION] Review-generation campaign** — 33 Google reviews against 454 Justdial ratings is a fixable, high-value gap.

---

### Pillar 5 — Design & architecture

**Purpose:** justify premium pricing through design intelligence.
**Formats:** architectural walkthroughs, design-decision explainers, WOW! Architects collaboration content.
**Platform:** Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn.
**KPI:** engagement quality, brand search lift.

**Concepts:** *"Why the Terraces Are That Deep"* (passive design explained) · *"Designing for 42°C"* · WOW! Architects interview.

---

### Pillar 6 — Leadership & company

**Purpose:** trust through visible, credible leadership.
**Formats:** founder interviews, LinkedIn commentary, press, HEALTHWAY content.
**Platform:** LinkedIn, YouTube, PR.
**KPI:** press pickups, LinkedIn engagement, partner enquiries.

**[INFERENCE]** The founder already earns national press (*The Hindu*, on HEALTHWAY sustainable mobility) on a theme that maps precisely onto the company's product differentiation, with no connection between the two. Linking them is free equity: a founder who advocates sustainable urban living **and** built India's first net-zero villa community is a coherent, credible story that no regional competitor can replicate.

## 4. Repeatable campaign formats

**[VERIFIED]** The January 2024 *"Capture the Tallest Building in Vijayawada"* photo contest (₹10,000 prize) is the one documented KMV campaign — smart, cheap, participatory, locally-reaching, and never repeated.

**[RECOMMENDATION]** Systematise it into a quarterly programme:
- **Q1** — "Capture the Skyline" (landmark, city-wide reach)
- **Q2** — "My Vivaan Story" (resident UGC, doubles as testimonial harvesting)
- **Q3** — "Watch It Rise" (TRAYA construction progress contest — turns delivery transparency into engagement)
- **Q4** — "Home for the Holidays" (NRI-timed, December visit window)

**[INFERENCE]** Each generates user content, local reach, and email/phone capture at trivial cost, while reinforcing a different pillar. The company already proved the format works; it simply never ran it again.

## 5. Production model

**[OBSERVED]** KMV hires in-house 3D visualisers, architects and graphic designers — real production capacity exists.

**[RECOMMENDATION]** In-house: photography, progress films, social content, design assets. Outsourced: long-form SEO writing, PR, high-end brand films. Founder-led: thought leadership, interviews.

**[RECOMMENDATION] Sequence matters.** Before producing anything new, fix distribution of what exists: re-title and re-describe the YouTube library for search, add end screens and links, build playlists by project and by buyer question, and embed videos on landing pages. **Distribution of existing assets is cheaper and faster than new production**, and it is where the immediate return sits.
