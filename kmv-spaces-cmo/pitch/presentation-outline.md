## Delivery notes

**If you get 10 minutes instead of 40:** slides 01, 05, 07, 15, 16, 18, 25. That sequence carries the entire argument — rare substance, honest market read, the structural brand problem, the commercial reframe, the budget insight, the TRAYA call, and the close.

**The three slides that prove seniority:** **05** (disagreeing with the obvious Amaravati story), **15** (CPL optimisation destroys value at this AOV), and **16** (funnel quality is the budget lever). Anyone can list marketing gaps; these three show commercial judgement.

**Handling "where did you get this?":** every number is sourced in `/research/sources.md`. Say plainly that internal performance data is unknown to you and that the 30-day plan exists to replace assumption with fact. **Volunteering the limits of your analysis is more persuasive than hiding them** — and it's the difference between a consultant's pitch and a colleague's assessment.
