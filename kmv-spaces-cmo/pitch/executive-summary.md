# Executive Summary

**KMV Spaces — Marketing & Growth Audit, August 2026**

---

## The situation in one paragraph

KMV Spaces has built genuinely rare product substance — **India's first Net Zero Energy residential villa community**, **Andhra Pradesh's first IGBC Platinum pre-certified community**, a completed project rated **4.2/5 across 454 public ratings**, and a parent group that builds AIIMS campuses, IIM Indore, airports and the state's own landmark tower. **[VERIFIED]** No competitor in the region holds an equivalent green certification. And almost none of this is doing commercial work: there is no indexed blog, no visible PR programme, brand equity sits in a project rather than the company, and the brand is scattered across seven-plus digital identities — one of them an Instagram account with **481 followers and zero posts**. The gap is not product. It is not even demand. It is the machinery between them.

---

## Five findings

**1. The monopoly asset is invisible.** Two first-in-category certifications, uncontested regionally, generating no measurable demand.

**2. Equity is trapped one level too low.** 454 ratings for *Vivaan*; 33 Google reviews for *KMV Spaces*. TRAYA must launch as *"Makers of Vivaan"* — borrowing from a project, not a company. Every launch restarts credibility from zero.

**3. Seven-plus fragmented identities.** Three Facebook pages, two Instagram handles (one dead), a separate microsite, a dormant X account. Audience, pixel data, reviews and search authority all split. **Cost to fix: near zero.**

**4. The funnel is unmeasured.** CRM status, speed-to-lead, cost per booking — all *Not publicly available*. Modelling shows a **29× difference in required media spend** between a weak and a strong funnel for identical revenue.

**5. Distribution is uncontrolled.** A broker published TRAYA's pre-launch details before KMV's own channels did, with their own phone number attached.

---

## The commercial reframe

**[VERIFIED]** Average booking value ≈ **₹3 crore**.

At that ticket size, conventional digital optimisation destroys value. A campaign with **5× the CPL** but better qualification and site-visit rates produces **₹21 crore more revenue on identical spend**. Sixty bookings a year is a **₹180 Cr** business needing roughly **2,000–4,000 qualified leads** — not tens of thousands.

**Lead volume is not the constraint. Lead quality and sales conversion are.**

Marketing must therefore be measured on **cost per booking**, never CPL — and at 1.5–2% of revenue, direct marketing is already cost-competitive with channel-partner commissions that typically run 2%+.

---

## The market window

**[VERIFIED]** Capital construction relaunched May 2025; **₹57,821 Cr** of works in progress; assembly due 2028. Capital-region prices **doubled in six months**. Tadepalli — TRAYA's location — averages **₹5,653/sq ft**, up **34.2% over three years**, with villas appreciating **~3× faster than apartments** and premium supply limited.

**[INFERENCE] But the honest read:** what re-rated is *land*, bought by speculators and NRIs. A ₹3 Cr finished villa is a different product for a different buyer. Positioning TRAYA on capital appreciation puts it into an unwinnable fight against ₹40 lakh plots. **Amaravati is the timing argument, not the demand thesis.**

Meanwhile Aparna — a far larger brand — is now **physically adjacent** to TRAYA. The window to establish category leadership cheaply is measured in quarters.

---

## Five strategic moves

| # | Move | Cost | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Consolidate the brand** — one identity, endorsed architecture ("KMV Spaces presents TRAYA") | Near zero | Compounding |
| **2** | **Rebuild the funnel before scaling spend** — CRM, <5 min speed-to-lead, fast landing pages, sales SLA | Low | **Largest single lever** |
| **3** | **Turn certifications into a demand engine** — quantified running-cost content, PR, category authority | Low–med | High |
| **4** | **Launch TRAYA on proof, not speculation** — *proven builders, future-proof homes, in the capital region* | Med | Very high |
| **5** | **Professionalise the two owned channels** — channel partners and existing residents | Low | **Cheapest demand available** |

---

## Why the sequence matters

**[VERIFIED]** Responding to an enquiry within 5 minutes converts to site visits at **4× the rate** of responding within an hour. **[VERIFIED]** Landing pages under 2 seconds typically cut CPL **20–35%**.

Neither requires media budget. Both multiply the value of every rupee spent afterwards. **This is why measurement and consolidation precede campaigns** — scaling spend into an unmeasured funnel is how developers lose money without learning why.

---

## The five KPIs

**Bookings · Cost per booking · Site visits completed · Speed-to-lead · Qualified lead rate**

Deliberately absent: impressions, reach, followers, likes, CPL. None tell a promoter whether the company sold a house.

---

## What this document does not claim

KMV's internal marketing performance, spend, lead volumes, conversion rates, CAC, CRM data, sales capacity, TRAYA pricing and RERA status, and buyer-origin data are all ***Not publicly available***. Every funnel rate used in modelling is labelled **[ASSUMPTION]**; every benchmark is cited as a benchmark, never as KMV's performance.

**No budget should be committed on this document's numbers — only on its structure.** The 30-day plan exists precisely to replace those assumptions with facts.

---

## The one-sentence thesis

> KMV Spaces has spent ten years earning trust it has never learned to spend — and the capital region is about to make attention abundant and trust scarce, which is either the company's best commercial opportunity or the moment a better-marketed competitor takes the position it already deserves.
