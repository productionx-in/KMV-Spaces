## The compounding logic

**[INFERENCE]** The roadmap is deliberately back-loaded on brand and front-loaded on plumbing. The reason is commercial, not aesthetic:

- **Q1 work** (measurement, consolidation, speed) produces no visible campaign but **determines the efficiency of every rupee spent in Q2–Q4**. The reverse-funnel model showed a 29× spend difference between a weak and strong funnel.
- **Q2 work** (TRAYA launch, endorsed brand) is where revenue impact begins, landing largely in Q3 given a 3–9 month sales cycle.
- **Q3–Q4 work** (category authority, referral, partner depth) builds assets that **reduce acquisition cost for every future project** — which is the only durable answer to competing against better-capitalised rivals like Aparna.

**[RECOMMENDATION]** The single most important thing to protect in this roadmap is Q1. It is the quarter with the least to show and the most to determine. If leadership pressure forces campaign spend into Q1 before measurement exists, the entire year's efficiency case collapses — and that argument should be made explicitly in week one, not defended retroactively in month four.
