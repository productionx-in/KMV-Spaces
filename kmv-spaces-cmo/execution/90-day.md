## Week 10–11 — Site visit as a conversion product

**[INFERENCE]** With ~₹3 Cr at stake, the site visit is where the sale is actually made. Treating it as a scheduling task rather than a designed experience wastes the most expensive lead in the funnel.

### The designed journey

| Stage | What happens | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| **Confirmation** | WhatsApp within 60s: time, map pin, RM name and photo, what to expect, who to bring | Automated |
| **Reminder** | T-24h and T-2h WhatsApp; RM call T-24h | Automated + RM |
| **Arrival** | Named parking, greeted by name, refreshments | Site team |
| **Framing** | 5 min: KMV Group story — AIIMS, IIM, airports, APNRT tower | RM |
| **Proof** | **Walk the completed Vivaan community** — real homes, real residents | RM |
| **Location** | Map session: schools, hospitals, commute, capital works status — honest about what's built vs announced | RM |
| **Product** | Model/show unit, floor plans, materials, specification | RM |
| **The numbers** | Net-zero savings sheet — rupees per year, over 20 years | RM |
| **Objections** | Price, water, timeline addressed proactively with the one-pagers | RM |
| **Commercial** | Payment plan, finance options, current availability | RM |
| **Close** | Clear next step and date — never "we'll call you" | RM |
| **Follow-up** | Same-day thank-you + call referencing their specific concern | RM + automation |

| # | Action | Owner | Priority | KPI |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 59 | Document and roll out the site-visit playbook | CMO + Sales | **P0** | Visit→booking rate |
| 60 | Train RMs on the playbook and objection handling | Sales Head | P0 | Consistency |
| 61 | Implement visit feedback logging (same day, structured) | Sales | P0 | Feedback 100% |

**[RECOMMENDATION]** What the customer should *feel*: that this developer is unusually organised, unusually honest about risk, and has nothing to hide — because they were shown a finished community rather than a rendering. That feeling is the actual product differentiator at the point of decision.

**[RECOMMENDATION]** The honest framing for leadership — this is a 90-day *foundation*, not a 90-day revenue miracle. Realistic markers:

| Marker | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Speed-to-lead under 5 minutes, sustained | **[VERIFIED]** 4× site-visit conversion |

| Referral and partner channels producing measurably | Cheapest demand activated |

**[INFERENCE]** The bookings impact of this quarter's work lands mostly in months 4–9, because the sales cycle is 3–9 months. **That lag must be stated to promoters in week one**, or good foundational work gets judged against a timeline it was never going to meet — which is how marketing functions get defunded one quarter before they start compounding.
