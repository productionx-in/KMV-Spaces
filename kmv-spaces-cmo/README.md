# KMV Spaces — CMO Audit & Growth Strategy

An evidence-based marketing and commercial audit of KMV Spaces (KMV Group), built from public sources as of **August 2026**, plus a growth strategy for the next 12 months.

## Evidence convention

Every material claim in this project carries one of four labels. This matters: a strategy that quietly mixes fact with assumption is how bad capital decisions get made.

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **[VERIFIED]** | Confirmed in a primary or credible secondary source, cited inline. |
| **[OBSERVED]** | Directly visible in a public asset (a page, a profile, a listing) at time of audit. Reported as seen, not interpreted. |
| **[INFERENCE]** | A conclusion drawn from verified/observed evidence. Reasoning is stated so it can be challenged. |
| **[ASSUMPTION]** | A working number or premise used to build a model. Must be replaced with real internal data before it drives a budget. |
| **Not publicly available** | Stated explicitly rather than guessed. These become the internal audit list on day one. |

**What this document does not do:** it does not claim to know KMV's internal marketing performance, CAC, lead volumes, conversion rates, budgets, CRM data, or campaign results. None of that is public. Where those numbers are needed, this project supplies the *model* and names the *variable to plug in*.

## A note on research access

Direct fetching of `kmvspaces.com` and the major social platforms was blocked by network policy in the research environment. Findings were reconstructed from search-index data, third-party property portals, and screenshots of the live site and profiles. Anything unverifiable is labelled as such rather than filled in.

## Structure

```
/research/     What is actually true about the company, market, and competitors
/strategy/     What to do about it, and why
/execution/    Sequenced plans — 30/60/90 day, 12-month roadmap
/pitch/        Interview-ready material and presentation outline
/data/         Matrices in CSV for spreadsheet work
```

**Read in this order:** `pitch/executive-summary.md` → `research/traya.md` → `strategy/executive-strategy.md` → `strategy/marketing-economics.md`. That path carries the argument. Everything else is supporting depth.

## The one-line thesis

KMV Spaces has built genuine, rare product substance — India's first Net Zero Energy villa community and AP's first IGBC Platinum pre-certified community — and has almost no commercial machinery converting that substance into demand. The gap between what the product deserves and what the marketing delivers is the entire opportunity, and TRAYA's launch into a re-rating Amaravati market is the moment it becomes expensive to ignore.
