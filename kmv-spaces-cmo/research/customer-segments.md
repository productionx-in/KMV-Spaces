# Customer Segmentation

## Method note

**[ASSUMPTION]** This segmentation is built from market structure, product price point, and regional dynamics — **not** from KMV's actual buyer data, which is *Not publicly available*. The first internal request would be a **buyer-origin and reason-for-purchase analysis of Vivaan's ~66 villa families and apartment owners**. That single dataset would confirm or overturn the priority ranking below, and it already exists inside the company.

**Segments deliberately excluded:** first-time buyers, affordable/mid-segment buyers, and pure rental-yield investors. At ₹2.95–3.8 Cr **[VERIFIED]**, these segments cannot transact and marketing to them wastes budget and pollutes lead quality.

---

## Segment 1 — Existing KMV residents upgrading *(highest yield)*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who** | Vivaan apartment owners moving to a villa; villa owners buying a second unit or for children |
| **Size** | Small — a few hundred households **[INFERENCE]** |
| **Income proxy** | Already demonstrated ₹1–4 Cr purchasing capacity |
| **Motivation** | Life-stage upgrade; family expansion; buying for adult children |
| **Trigger** | New launch announcement; children marrying; business liquidity event |
| **Objection** | "Why move within the same developer?"; delay memory from Vivaan |
| **Information need** | Loyalty pricing, upgrade path, comparison against their current home |
| **Channel** | **Direct — RM call, WhatsApp, resident events.** Not paid media. |
| **Timeline** | Short (weeks–months) — trust already established |
| **Decision unit** | Family; spouse is usually decisive |
| **CTA** | "Private preview for KMV residents, before public launch" |

**[INFERENCE]** This is the cheapest revenue available to the company and there is no public evidence it is being worked. These buyers have already paid, already lived in the product, and already trust delivery. In premium real estate, existing customers and their referrals routinely constitute a large share of bookings at a fraction of blended CAC. **Priority: highest ROI, lowest cost, fastest to activate.**

## Segment 2 — Vijayawada / Guntur affluent end-users *(core volume)*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who** | Business owners, doctors, industrialists, senior professionals, established local families |
| **Income proxy** | ₹40 L+ household income or significant business assets |
| **Motivation** | Status address; space; community; children's environment |
| **Trigger** | Business growth; outgrowing current home; peer purchase |
| **Objection** | Price; delay risk; "is Tadepalli better than where I am now?" |
| **Information need** | Build quality, delivery record, neighbours' profile, school/hospital access, resale |
| **Channel** | Meta (geo + interest), Google Search (high-intent), **referral**, channel partners, outdoor near the site, local press |
| **Timeline** | 3–9 months |
| **Decision unit** | Family — spouse and often parents |
| **CTA** | "Visit the completed community" — Vivaan site visit as proof |

**[INFERENCE]** The volume backbone. Critically, this buyer can *drive to Vivaan and see a finished KMV community* — the single most persuasive asset the company owns. Marketing's job for this segment is not persuasion, it is **getting them physically to a completed site**.

## Segment 3 — Hyderabad-based AP-origin buyers *(high value)*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who** | Professionals and business owners with family roots in Vijayawada/Guntur; often IT, pharma, business |
| **Motivation** | Second home; retirement planning; proximity to ageing parents; emotional connection to home region |
| **Trigger** | Parents ageing; capital-region news; a Hyderabad property becoming unaffordable |
| **Objection** | "I don't live there — who manages it?"; distance; liquidity |
| **Information need** | Property management, rental potential, capital-region credibility, remote purchase process |
| **Channel** | **Meta lookalike targeting Hyderabad**, LinkedIn, Google Search, Hyderabad-based events |
| **Timeline** | 6–12 months |
| **Decision unit** | Individual + extended family |
| **CTA** | "See it without travelling" — video walkthrough, virtual tour, then a planned weekend visit |

**[INFERENCE]** Strategically important because **[INFERENCE from `/research/market.md` §6]** the local ₹3 Cr end-user pool is likely narrower than the price movement implies, given a thin private employment base. Out-of-region demand is not optional; it is structurally necessary. KMV's Hyderabad office is a real advantage here — it can host and convert this segment without them travelling.

## Segment 4 — NRIs *(high value, infrastructure-dependent)*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who** | AP-origin NRIs, primarily US/Gulf |
| **Motivation** | **[VERIFIED]** favourable rupee exchange rate; emotional connection; retirement/legacy planning; parents' housing |
| **Trigger** | India visit (Dec–Jan, festivals); rupee movement; capital-region news reaching diaspora |
| **Objection** | Trust at distance; "who handles it?"; repatriation, FEMA, tax; can't inspect |
| **Information need** | RERA compliance, NRI purchase process, remote documentation, property management, video proof, **verified developer credibility** |
| **Channel** | Meta geo-targeted to US/Gulf AP diaspora, YouTube, WhatsApp, diaspora associations, referral from existing residents |
| **Timeline** | 6–18 months, often closing during a physical visit |
| **Decision unit** | NRI + India-based family member who inspects on their behalf |
| **CTA** | "Book a video walkthrough with our team at your time zone" |

**[OBSERVED]** KMV already publishes a **USA toll-free number** — evidence of intent — but no NRI-specific content, campaign, or process is publicly visible.

**[INFERENCE]** This segment is either a real opportunity or an unmanaged promise, and right now it is the latter. The decisive factor is not advertising — it is **remote-purchase infrastructure**: video walkthroughs, a documented NRI process, time-zone-aware response, and a named relationship manager. **[VERIFIED]** the rupee advantage and RERA transparency make 2026 structurally favourable for NRI investment. Also note the December–January India-visit window is the natural campaign peak.

## Segment 5 — HNI second-home / legacy buyers *(low volume, low price sensitivity)*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who** | High-net-worth families buying for children, legacy, or portfolio diversification |
| **Motivation** | Legacy; diversification; prestige |
| **Objection** | Exclusivity, community quality, "who are the neighbours" |
| **Information need** | Design pedigree, buyer profile curation, discretion |
| **Channel** | **Referral, private events, wealth managers, channel partners** — not mass media |
| **Timeline** | Variable |
| **CTA** | Private, invitation-only preview |

**[INFERENCE]** Low volume but disproportionate value, and the segment most responsive to WOW! Architects' design pedigree and the net-zero story as markers of taste rather than economics. Reached through relationships, not campaigns.

---

## Deliberately deprioritised

**Pure capital-region speculators.** They exist and they have money — **[VERIFIED]** capital-region prices doubled in six months on NRI and institutional plot demand. But they buy **plots, not ₹3 Cr villas** (reasoning in `/research/market.md` §2). Chasing them generates high lead volume, low qualification rates, and price-anchoring against land. **[RECOMMENDATION] Exclude from targeting; use capital-region growth as a supporting confidence argument to end-users instead.**

## What internal data would sharpen this

*Not publicly available* — and each materially changes budget allocation:

1. **Vivaan buyer origin** — the single most valuable dataset. If 70% came from within 15 km, Segment 2 dominates spend. If 30% came from Hyderabad or overseas, Segments 3–4 justify serious investment.
2. **Referral share of past bookings** — sizes the Segment 1 opportunity
3. **Lost-lead reasons** — reveals which objection actually kills deals
4. **Enquiry-to-booking rate by source** — the only honest basis for channel budget allocation
5. **Whether the USA toll-free line has ever generated a booking** — settles the NRI question in one query
