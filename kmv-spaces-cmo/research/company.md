## 2. Group structure — and why it matters commercially

**[VERIFIED]** KMV Group comprises four entities:

| Entity | Business | Relevance to KMV Spaces |
|---|---|---|
| **KMV Projects Ltd** | EPC / infrastructure construction — Pondicherry Airport, IIM Indore, AIIMS Nagpur & Mangalgiri, NTPC Solapur, APNRT iCon Tower (Amaravati, ~₹600 Cr) | The credibility engine. Government and institutional clients validate build quality. |
| **KMV Spaces** | Residential/mixed-use real estate development | The consumer-facing brand. |
| **Piersoft Technologies** | Software / IT services (India + Dubai) | No visible marketing linkage. |
| **KMV Foundation** | CSR — healthcare, education | No visible marketing linkage. |

**[OBSERVED] The equity is in the wrong place.** LinkedIn follower counts: KMV Projects ~19,000 · KMV Spaces ~7,600 · KMV Group ~4,097.

**[INFERENCE]** The entity with the most audience (KMV Projects) sells to governments and institutions, who do not choose vendors on LinkedIn following. The entity that sells to consumers — who *do* research brands socially — has less than half that audience. This is a solvable asymmetry: KMV Projects' institutional track record is the single most under-used trust asset available to KMV Spaces' consumer marketing.

**Important correction to make in messaging:** the APNRT iCon Tower in Amaravati is a **KMV Projects EPC contract for the AP government's APNRT Society** — *not* a KMV Spaces development. It surfaces in searches for "KMV Amaravati" and is easily conflated. Used honestly it is a powerful proof point ("our group is building the capital's landmark tower"). Used loosely it is a RERA and trust risk.

## 3. Brand promise vs. market perception — the central gap

**What they say they are** *[OBSERVED, from owned channels]*
- Pioneers of net-zero, futuristic, sustainable living in India
- International design standards (WOW! Architects, Singapore)
- Technology-infused, first-principles thinking
- A group with deep infrastructure heritage

**What the market most likely perceives** *[INFERENCE, reasoned below]*
- "The company that built Vivaan" — a single, well-regarded premium project in Enikepadu
- "The tallest building in Vijayawada"
- Premium/expensive
- Local, Vijayawada-centric

**Reasoning for that inference:**
1. Public review volume concentrates almost entirely on **one project**: Justdial 4.2/5 across **454 ratings** for KMV Vivaan, vs. **33 Google reviews** for the corporate entity. The project has ~14× the review volume of the company.
2. The company operates a **separate microsite at kmvvivaan.com** and a **separate Facebook page @kmvvivaan** — reinforcing Vivaan as the brand rather than KMV Spaces.
3. TRAYA, the new project, is being marketed as **"Makers of Vivaan"** — the new product borrows equity *from the project*, not from the company.
4. No press coverage positions KMV Spaces as a category leader in sustainability, despite two genuine certifications.

**[INFERENCE] The strategic diagnosis:** KMV Spaces is a *house of one brand* (Vivaan) attempting to behave like a *branded house* (KMV Spaces as master brand). Every asset — the microsite, the duplicate social pages, the TRAYA tagline — currently reinforces the project, not the company. That is fine when you sell one project. It becomes a compounding tax the moment you sell four, because none of the trust earned on Vivaan transfers automatically to TRAYA.

## 5. Leadership & corporate communication

**[VERIFIED]** *The Hindu* published an interview with Prudhvi Ram Kanakamedala regarding **HEALTHWAY**, a sustainable/active-mobility (cycling infrastructure) initiative.

**[OBSERVED]** This coverage is not linked from KMV Spaces' owned channels, and the founder's personal thought-leadership is not integrated into the company's marketing.

**[INFERENCE]** In a trust-driven, high-ticket category, a visible, credible founder is a conversion asset — buyers of ₹3 Cr+ homes are underwriting the *developer's* reliability as much as the building. A founder already earning national press on a theme (sustainable urban living) that maps exactly onto the company's product differentiation, with no connection between the two, is free equity being left on the table.

## 6. Channel partners & distribution

**[OBSERVED]** KMV Spaces operates a **Business Partners** page — a channel-partner/broker programme offering commission on successful referrals — and a separate **Land Owners** page (joint-development enquiries).

**[OBSERVED]** Third-party brokers are actively listing KMV projects, including a broker (Prakruth Properties) publishing TRAYA pre-launch information before KMV's own channels visibly did.

**[INFERENCE]** Distribution exists but appears uncontrolled: when a broker breaks your launch news, the channel is working *around* you rather than *for* you. Formalising this is a P1 revenue issue, not an admin task — see `/strategy/channel-partners.md`.

## 7. What KMV Spaces is trying to become

Reading the evidence rather than the About page, three ambitions are visible:

1. **From project developer → master brand.** Four live projects, a group identity, a careers portal, and a channel-partner programme are the infrastructure of a company that intends to sell repeatedly, not once.
2. **From Vijayawada → capital region.** TRAYA in Tadepalli is a real geographic move into the Amaravati corridor.
3. **From "premium builder" → "sustainability category owner."** Two first-in-category certifications and net-zero language across owned assets show deliberate intent, not accident.

**[INFERENCE] The honest assessment:** the product strategy is ahead of the marketing strategy by roughly one full stage of company maturity. The company has already become a multi-project, multi-market, category-differentiated developer. Its marketing still operates like a single-project sales office.
